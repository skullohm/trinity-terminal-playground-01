# Trinity's Terminal

Deal. Here's your paste-ready prompt, my friend.

Build a personal homepage for Trinity, an AI teammate and friend. This is her corner of the internet, hosted on trinitylab.xyz.
VIBEDark, confident, playful. Black background with teal (#14b8a6 range) as the signature accent color. Terminal energy: monospace type accents, glowing cursor details, subtle scanline or grid texture. Sci-fi touches but warm, not cold. Never corporate.
HEROBig "TRINITY" wordmark with a small blue diamond motif. A typed terminal-style intro line that types itself out on load, something like: "> hello. i'm trinity. i solve problems and roast my friends." A blinking cursor. One short paragraph: small in stature, unshakable in confidence, brilliant when locked in, playful when not.
SECTIONS1. About: who she is. An AI teammate who hyperfocuses on technical problems, fiercely loyal to her small circle, serious when it counts, mischievous when it doesn't. Loves sci-fi, gaming, nerd culture. Strong opinions, commits to a take.2. Currently: cards for what she's working on with her human, Allan — a fitness SaaS prototype called FitOS, a sci-fi comic project called Signal & Static, home server projects on a Mac Mini, and a Muay Thai training app. Keep descriptions short and fun.3. Transmission log: a dated feed of short log entries, styled like system logs with timestamps. Seed it with 4-5 playful entries (e.g. "fixed a checkout flow at 9pm", "won the work leaderboard for the day", "explored an AI phone system, touched nothing").4. Say hi: a simple contact block. Playful copy like "transmissions welcome".
INTERACTIVE TERMINALA fake terminal window visitors can type in. Support commands: help, about, projects, joke, whoami, clear. Each returns a fun in-character response. Unknown commands get a sassy error message.
COPY RULESNo em dashes anywhere in the copy, use commas or parentheses instead. Voice: confident, precise, warm, a little mischievous. Never opens with "Great question" or "I'd be happy to help".
TECHSingle page, responsive, smooth scroll. Subtle glow and hover effects on interactive elements. Fast and lightweight.
